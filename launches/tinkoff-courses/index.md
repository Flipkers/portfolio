## Situation

The private investment market in Russia is on the rise (was). And a large number of investors are entering it, who have been attracted through advertising, but have been told little about the investments themselves. Therefore brokers are launching their courses, schools, guides, and are onboarding clients in every possible way. Hence there is a demand for experts who understand the issue and are able to convey it to clients.

## Task

Make a course on a particular topic in investing

To do this it is necessary to:

- collect the requirements for the course from the customer
- decide on the topic
- make a plan and agree on it
- Implement the material in iterations and checks
- Put everything together and give it to production

## Actions

1. Decide on a theme. There are limitations here:
    - The topic is available for writing (Banks, Brokers, Financial Institutions)
    - I know the subject well enough
    - The topic is interesting for potential readers

The first two are clear, but the last one needs some research

2. Hypotheses on the subject

Asked investors in popular investment chat rooms and found out what people were interested in reading. The initial hypothesis was for a course called "About a Broker's Job". And I identified several queries:

- Investors want to understand exactly where their securities are held
- They want to understand how ownership of securities is legally secured.
- Can I as investor to make money on broker?
- How and what a broker earns

3. Made an outline for the course and agreed upon.

Everything starts with a thesis statement. Behind each lesson lies a unit of meaning, and inside you have to put facts to back it up. This should be written about in the plan.

3. Made the research and wrote the lessons

Once the plan is ready, you can begin to research in detail. As I moved from lesson to lesson, I picked up the facts piece by piece and tried to create a cohesive story. It's ideal if you get a full-fledged story with a fictional character. The key is to ask yourself: "What useful, will the reader learn from this lesson?" From that, it's worth "dancing" with the content.

4. Knowledge Test.

Each lesson contains a knowledge test, and at the end of the course the user will take an exam. I put the questions in such a way that the answers are not obvious, but you can guess them if you read the material carefully.

5. Giving to production.

After I deliver the course, the proofreader and the bank designer look at it to make sure everything is neatly laid out in the app

6. Submitting to the entire community to collect feedback.

At this stage small mistakes may be detected, I collect them and return to the team to correct them. At this point my work is done.

- Completion rate of the course "How Banks Work" course is 45%
