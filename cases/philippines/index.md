# Market Research on Payment Provider Regulations in the Philippines

I was engaged by a consulting boutique from the MENA region to expand their market expertise into Asia, specifically the Philippines. My task involved identifying data sources from both companies and government agencies that aggregate publicly accessible financial information. I conducted an in-depth analysis of the Philippine market, focusing on central bank regulations concerning payment provider licenses. This included assessing the number of new payment providers, licensing requirements, associated costs, and application procedures. ### **Problem**

A consulting boutique from the **MENA region** wanted to expand its expertise beyond its core markets and gain insights into **Asia, specifically the Philippines**. They needed a **comprehensive understanding of the regulatory landscape** for fintech and payment providers, including:

- **Availability of licenses** for new payment providers.
- **Regulatory requirements** (costs, application process, restrictions).
- **Market saturation** (how many new payment providers were entering the space).

To achieve this, they required an **in-depth market screening** using data from **central banks, regulatory agencies, and public databases**—a task that required specialized fintech knowledge and extensive research skills.

### **Action**

I conducted a **deep-dive research project** into the Philippine fintech market by:

1. **Identifying and Analyzing Public Data Sources**
    - Explored **government and company databases** aggregating financial market data.
    - Researched **Central Bank of the Philippines’ regulations** on fintech licensing.
2. **Evaluating Licensing Structures**
    - Collected data on **payment provider licenses**, including:
        - **License types** required for operations.
        - **Application costs and procedures**.
        - **Regulatory compliance requirements**.
3. **Assessing Market Entry Feasibility**
    - Screened the market to determine **how many new payment providers** were entering.
    - Compared licensing models with **other Southeast Asian markets** to benchmark opportunities.

### **Result**

- Delivered a **comprehensive market assessment** that expanded the firm’s knowledge beyond MENA into Asia.
- Provided insights into the **licensing framework and competitive landscape** for payment providers in the Philippines.
- Enabled the consulting boutique to make **informed strategic decisions** on fintech expansion opportunities.
